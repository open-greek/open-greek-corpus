An asterisk (*) prefixed to a reference indicates a double protasis. In I 358 foll. and 379 foll. the mark indicates a triple, and in δ 222 it indicates a quadruple protasis.—Note that as, for the purpose of the Table, it has sometimes been necessary to invert the order of the protasis and apodosis, the rules under οἷ (3) (a) and μή (6) (c) will in the cases of such inversion apparently not hold.
